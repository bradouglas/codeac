## The admin that eats your week

Most small business owners know automation could help them. The problem is knowing where to start.

After working with dozens of UK businesses, we have found the same five tasks come up again and again. They are not glamorous. But fixing them frees up meaningful amounts of time every single week.

Here they are, in order of impact.

## 1. Invoice generation and chasing

Manual invoicing is one of the most common time drains we see. Business owners spend hours each month generating invoices, then more hours chasing the ones that do not get paid on time.

The fix is simple: connect your project management tool or CRM to your accounting software. When a project is marked complete, an invoice goes out automatically. When the due date passes without payment, reminders go out at 7 days, 14 days, and 30 days.

Tools that work well here: Xero, QuickBooks, Stripe, Make.

**Time saved: 3 to 5 hours per month.**

## 2. Lead follow-ups

The average response time for inbound leads in the UK is over 47 hours. That is almost two full days. By then, many prospects have moved on.

Automating your follow-up sequence means every new lead gets a response within minutes. Not a generic email, but a personalised one based on what they asked about. Your CRM is updated automatically, and a task is created for you to do the human bit.

Tools that work well here: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zapier, Make.

**Time saved: 2 to 4 hours per week.**

## 3. Client onboarding

Every time you sign a new client, there is a checklist of things that need to happen. Welcome email. Contract sent. Account created. Intro call booked. Files shared.

If you are doing this manually every time, you are doing a job a computer should be doing.

An automated onboarding sequence handles all of this the moment a new client is added to your CRM. Everything goes out in the right order, at the right time, without you lifting a finger.

**Time saved: 1 to 2 hours per new client.**

## 4. Reporting and data entry

If you are manually copying data between tools, from your CRM into a spreadsheet, or from a spreadsheet into a report, you are doing work a computer does better than you.

Automated reporting pulls data from your tools on a set schedule and builds the report for you. Your Monday morning dashboard is waiting in your inbox before you sit down.

Tools that work well here: Google Sheets, Zapier, Make, Airtable.

**Time saved: 2 to 3 hours per week.**

## 5. Appointment reminders

No-shows cost you money. Chasing clients to confirm appointments costs you time. An automated reminder sequence handles both.

The moment an appointment is booked, a confirmation goes out. 24 hours before, a reminder. An hour before, another one. Rescheduling is handled with a link, not a phone call.

**Time saved: 30 minutes to 1 hour per day for appointment-heavy businesses.**

## Where to start

Pick the one that costs you the most time right now. Build that first. See the results. Then move on to the next one.
