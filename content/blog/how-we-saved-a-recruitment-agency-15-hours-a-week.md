## The problem

A recruitment agency in London came to us with a problem we see all the time: they were growing, but the growth was making life harder, not easier.

More clients meant more job briefs to process. More job briefs meant more candidate searches. More candidates meant more emails, more interview scheduling, more follow-ups, and more status updates. All of it manual. All of it done by the three-person team who were supposed to be recruiting.

By the time we spoke to them, the director was spending around four hours a day on tasks she described as "things a computer should be doing."

She was right.

## What we found

After an automation audit, we identified five core processes that were eating the team's time:

**1. Candidate status updates**
Every time a candidate moved through the pipeline, someone had to email them manually. At 20 to 30 candidates in the pipeline at any time, this alone was taking 90 minutes a day.

**2. Interview scheduling**
Coordinators were playing email ping-pong with candidates and clients to find a mutual slot. Each interview took an average of 8 emails to schedule.

**3. Job brief processing**
New job briefs came in by email. Someone had to read them, create a record in the CRM, assign it to a consultant, and send an acknowledgement to the client. Each one took about 20 minutes.

**4. Weekly client updates**
Every Friday, the team sent manual updates to each client showing where their vacancies stood. This took around three hours every week.

**5. Candidate document collection**
Collecting CVs, references, and compliance documents involved multiple manual follow-up emails per candidate.

## What we built

**Candidate updates:** We connected their ATS (applicant tracking system) to a Make workflow. Every time a candidate's stage changed, a personalised update email went out automatically, tailored to the new stage. No more manual emails.

**Interview scheduling:** We integrated Calendly with their ATS and email. When an interview needed to be booked, the system sent a scheduling link to both the candidate and client. When both confirmed, the calendar invite was created and the CRM updated. Eight emails became one.

**Job brief processing:** We built an email parser using AI that read incoming job briefs, extracted the key information, created the CRM record, assigned it to the right consultant based on specialism, and sent the client acknowledgement. Twenty minutes became thirty seconds.

**Weekly client reports:** We set up an automated report that pulled live data from the ATS every Friday morning, formatted it into a branded PDF, and sent it to each client automatically. Three hours became zero.

**Document collection:** We built an automated sequence that sent document requests in the right order, chased missing items at set intervals, and updated the CRM when everything was received.

## The results

After a four-week build and two weeks of testing, here is what changed:

- **15 hours per week** saved across the team
- **Candidate response time** dropped from 6 hours to under 10 minutes
- **Interview scheduling** went from an average of 8 emails to 1
- **Zero** late Friday reports
- **Director** reclaimed 3 hours every day she was using for status emails

The return on investment was visible within the first month. The Growth plan they were on paid for itself in time savings in week three.
